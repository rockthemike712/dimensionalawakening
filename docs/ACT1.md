# Act I: the plan

The course correction, applied. Nothing here is a rebuild. It is a
re-sequencing of what exists, a demotion, one hidden beat, and residue.

Thesis: the player never unlocks stronger abilities, only richer ways of
perceiving reality. North star: simple hands, deep mathematics, impossible
perception.

## B. Revised Act I flow

One continuous path east across the sheet, then north, then east, then
south. Nothing is hard-gated; the arrow suggests the order, the lights are
the only signposts, and the room is the one thing that does not exist until
the act is done.

**Residue** (the mechanics leave marks on the player):

- After Thin: every few steps the sphere flattens for a fraction of a second and pops back.
- After the Corner: a second, mirrored shadow flickers under the player now and then.
- After the Lamp: the world's awakening rises a step (colour drift, the stars spiral) and the shadow the player casts is a little brighter than black.

**Signposting**: the arrow points at Act I's next region in order (Thin, Corner, Lamp), never at the nearest. Every entrance light is visible from the start so a wandering player still finds them, but only the next one has its beam on.

**Act II hook**: when the Lamp is done, the two-gap wall, the screen and the emitter rise from the floor where the player first stood on the paper, with the room's chord. The arrow points there. Nothing is said.

## C. Implementation plan

Files:

- `src/game.js`
  - Act state derived from regions: `actDone = thin.done && corner.done && lamp.done`. Saved implicitly through region saves.
  - `collectSeed`: the third light no longer starts the room. Lights 2 and 3 move to (8, 6) and (13, −6) so they pull the player through the reeds.
  - The room region gets `buildWhen: () => actDone`, and `startStage2` plays the "grows from the floor" beat (group scale.y from 0 with the fold spring, ripples along the wall, the chord). The room's counter and eye button stay room-only, as now.
  - HUD: after the crossing the counter and pips hide unless the current region defines `hud()`. `PLACES n / 4` is removed. The 2D/3D label keeps saying the place's name.
  - Beacon: `nextRegion()` follows the order `thin → corner → lamp → room`, first not done. The arrow is suppressed until `digested` (60 s since crossing, or 40 units walked). 'Follow the lights.' appears only then.
  - Entrance lights: beam and glow only on the next region; others keep the core and ring.
  - Residue hooks: `residue.thin/corner/lamp` flags set from `done()`; the footstep flatten, the mirrored shadow flicker, the awakening bump.
- `src/regions/thin.js`, `tests/thin.mjs`: the revision in flight (corridor along +x, rule enforced, slot visible, twins ring without teleport, veil under the controls, fall with descent, pop overshoot).
- `src/regions/corner.js`, `tests/corner.mjs`: the revision in flight (drag axes, region-gated input, live light per order, marker under the active gate, same paper, reset on leave, mirror on the surface, no per-frame allocation).
- `src/regions/lamp.js`: remove `hud()`; the swap becomes the act's end: after it, the near-side figure stays as a bright, still shadow facing the player; `done()` when the far light is reached; the critic's fixes (in flight).
- `src/regions/beads.js`: only the critic's fixes, if cheap; otherwise it stays as is or is switched off.
- Untouched: the sheet shaders, the fold, the crossing, the reeds, save/continue, the region registry, the room's internals.
- Tests: `tests/smoke.mjs` shrinks to page → edge → crossing → the free-play beat (no arrow, no counter, then the arrow after the digest rule) → the Thin entrance. New `tests/act1.mjs`: jump through the three regions with `setPos`, assert the arrow order, the residues, that the room is absent until all three are done and present after, and Continue at both checkpoints. `tests/room.mjs`: unlock via debug hook, then the existing four patterns (moved out of smoke).
- Screenshots at 390×844: crossing +1 s, free play, each region's entrance from the field, the Thin squash, the Corner double fold, the Lamp swap, the room rising.

Order of work: merge the Thin and Corner revisions when their critics pass, then the core re-sequencing, then the Lamp trim, then the tests, then one full phone playthrough of the first fifteen minutes.

## D. Risks

- **The free-play beat reads as "nothing to do."** Mitigation: the two lights and the reeds are there to wander to; the digest rule is short (60 s or 40 units); the Thin light is lit from the start. If a tester stalls, shorten the rule, never add text.
- **Thin's overhead camera and the Corner's double fold at phone size.** Both critics flagged legibility. The revisions are judged again before merging; the bar is the critic's screenshot, not the test.
- **Two folds too similar.** The first edge and the Corner's edges use the same gesture; the Corner's drag axis change (down for the horizontal line, sideways for the vertical) must match the room's edge so the language stays one language.
- **Accidental progress.** A reed teleport or a wall-clamp bounce can carry the player past a slot or into a region. Thin's twins are fixed; the octave teleport stays but never lands inside a region's bounds (guard in `brushLandmarks`).
- **The identity crack over-explaining itself.** The Lamp has two prompts ("Pull the lamp down.", "Close your eyes."). Both stay fallback-only at 8 s. Nothing after the swap.
- **The room's late appearance confusing a returning player.** Continue restores `actDone`, so the room is either there or not, never half. The "grows from the floor" beat only plays once, on the transition.
- **Save state.** Act state is derived, not stored, so there is nothing new to migrate. A save made before this change has no region progress and resumes at the crossing point, which is correct.
- **Phone performance.** Four regions add roughly 30 draw calls and one extra shader plane. Measured headless frame rate fell from 11 to 7 fps under SwiftShader, which says nothing about an iPhone GPU, but the corner patch's ripple loop and the lamp's beam are the two things to watch. The reeds' point lights are hidden outside their regions.
- **Too much UI.** The counter is gone outside regions. If a region's own counter (SLOTS, CORNERS) starts feeling like a meter, it goes too; environmental confirmation (the light goes out, the corner unfolds) already exists for each.
